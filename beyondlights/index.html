What THE GRID does

Every feature on this page works in the development build we design shows with. The first packaged release carries fewer, and its list is published with the release.

A venue built to scale

Decks, truss, walls and boxes go where they go on the night, with move, resize, undo and redo. Fixtures placed in the builder take their own patch and refuse to collide.

  • 302 DMX fixtures over 6 universes and 44 lasers
  • A library of 204 venues and festival stages
  • Nine winch pods on keys

Every look before load-in

80 lighting recipes, 64 looks on the keyboard and 5 camera views. Shows save, autosave, open and recover as .gridshow files.

  • Recorded show videos from 6 views plus a montage
  • Auto, Balanced and Full detail, with a recording lock at a fixed resolution
  • Daylight, night sky and a lightning flash

Live from your console

Art-Net and sACN come in on 6 universes and merge highest takes precedence. The same rig you designed answers the desk.

  • A DMX monitor shows all 512 levels of a universe and exports them as CSV
  • A Wolfmix W1 over USB
  • A BEYOND visualization stream for native laser beams

Pyro, video and drones in the same room

Effects fire from the same channels as the lights, so a cue on the desk fires them in the model.

  • 20 stage flames, 20 roof flamers, 8 CO2 jets, 6 sonic booms, 8 confetti launchers, 9 fireworks racks and hall haze
  • Video walls play a file the right way round, and the venue's LED faces follow the look
  • A drone show of 1,224 drones

What about updates and later versions?
