Process

How a show runs

Four steps, all in writing.

  1. 01

    Quote

    Send the date, the venue and the crowd size. You get a written quote by email with the rig we recommend.

  2. 02

    Design and advance

    A 50% deposit holds the date. We send the rig plot, power needs and schedule, and a 3D model when the show calls for one.

  3. 03

    Show day

    We load in, hang and focus the rig, set the beam zones, and run the show live from the first act to the last.

  4. 04

    Strike

    We pack out the same night. The balance is due on show day.

Questions

Before you book

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as you have a date. Indoor shows need the least notice. Outdoor shows and rigs of 8 lasers or more take longer to plan, so allow at least 30 days.

What do you need from the venue?

Power, a place for the rig, and permission to run haze. The quote tells you how many circuits the rig needs. Lasers hang from truss or sit on stands, and we check sightlines and hang points with you before show day.

Is it safe for the crowd?

A certified laser safety officer sets the beam zones before doors and stays at the controls for the whole show. Beams are set to pass above the audience.

Can you just do the design, or just the lasers?

Yes. We take lasers-only shows alongside your lighting designer, full lighting and laser production, and design-only work: a 3D model, renders and a rig plot for your own crew to build from.

Where do you work?

We are based in the Phoenix area and work across Arizona. For shows outside the state, ask and we will tell you what travel adds.

What does it cost, and how does payment work?

Cost depends on the rig size, the venue and the date, so every show gets a written quote. A 50% deposit holds the date and the balance is due on show day.
