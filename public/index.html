How it works

Four clear steps, with every detail confirmed in writing.

  1. 01

    Quote

    Share your date, venue, and expected attendance. We’ll email you a written quote with the rig we recommend.

FAQ

Before you book

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as your date is confirmed. Indoor shows can often be booked on shorter notice. For outdoor shows and rigs of 8 or more lasers, allow at least 30 days, since outdoor laser shows require advance notice to the FAA.

What do you need from the venue?

Power, a location for the rig, and approval to run haze. Your quote lists the circuits the rig requires. Lasers can be flown from truss or mounted on stands, and we confirm sightlines and rigging points with your team before show day. If the venue has smoke detectors, we coordinate haze use with venue management in advance.

Is it safe for the audience?

Yes. A certified Laser Safety Officer sets and verifies every beam zone before doors and stays at the controls for the entire show. All beams are kept above the audience.

Can we book just the lasers, or just the design?

Yes. We offer lasers only, working alongside your lighting designer; full laser and lighting production; or design only: a 3D model, renders, and a lighting plot your crew can build from.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Phoenix and serve all of Arizona. Out-of-state shows are available, with travel quoted separately.

How much does a show cost?

Pricing depends on rig size, venue, and date, so every show receives a custom written quote. A 50% deposit secures your date, and the balance is due on the day of the event.
