Scope

What the design work includes

We design anything from a single stage for one night to a full venue. Book the complete package or only the parts you need.

  1. 01

    Venue and stage models

    We build to-scale 3D models of the venue, stage, and rig. We work from drawings, photos, or a site visit. Every fixture is modeled accurately.

  2. 02

    Show renders

    We render stills from the audience, the stage, and overhead. Each effect is shown on its own and combined.

  3. 03

    Looks

    We render the same rig in several looks. You pick the direction for each artist, night, or genre.

  4. 04

    Spec check

    We check the production spec against the model, line by line. Anything unconfirmed is flagged.

  5. 05

    Deliverables

    You receive final renders, the model file, and a fixture list your team can build from.
