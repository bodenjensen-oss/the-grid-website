THE GRID software

Design the show in 3D. Run it live.

THE GRID is our own Windows software. It models a venue to scale with the actual fixtures, renders every look before load-in, and then takes Art-Net or sACN from your console and runs the same rig live. We design every show on this site in it.

Features

What THE GRID does

Every item below works in the development build we design with. The first packaged release carries a smaller set, listed under build status.

  • Lasers

    44 lasers with scan looks, beam clipping on the venue geometry, far-beam width limits, and a hold whenever a winch pod drops into a beam. FB4 Max mode on universes 5 and 6.

  • Lighting

    302 DMX fixtures across 6 universes: colour mixing, dimmers and moving parts. A preset browser with 80 look recipes.

  • Pyro and effects

    20 stage flames, 20 roof flamers, 8 CO2 jets, 6 sonic booms, 8 confetti launchers, 9 fireworks racks, hall haze, and a drone show of 1,224 drones.

  • Video walls

    Video walls play a file the right way round. The venue's LED faces and logo follow the look channels.

  • Show control

    One icon starts the show. 64 looks on the keyboard, 5 camera views, 124 automatic fixture groups, a console on Tab, a festival controller in the window or popped out, and three premade shows.

  • Patch and files

    The show patch is written at start. Fixtures placed in the builder take their own patch, with collision refusal and undo. Shows save, autosave, open and recover as .gridshow files.

  • Rendering

    Auto, Balanced and Full detail, a recording lock at a fixed resolution, recorded show videos from 6 views plus a montage, daylight and night sky, and a lightning flash.

  • Hardware in and out

    Art-Net and sACN in on 6 universes, merged highest takes precedence. A Wolfmix W1 over USB. A BEYOND visualization stream for the lasers. A DMX monitor shows all 512 levels of a universe and exports them as CSV.

  • Venue and builder

    A to-scale venue map, decks, truss, walls and boxes with move, resize, delete, undo and redo, a library of 204 venues and festival stages, and nine winch pods on keys.

Build status

Where it stands

The feature list above is the development build, checked feature by feature on 16 November 2026. The packaged build from the same day carries 7 of those 58 features: the venue map, 302 fixtures over Art-Net in part, stage flames, CO2 jets, confetti, LED faces in part, and Art-Net input on 6 universes. A later development build adds the Live room shown above, with Art-Net and sACN inputs, the merge, outputs and the DMX monitor.

Previz as a service

We design your show in it

You do not need the software to get the result. Send us the venue and we model it, render the looks and check the spec before anything is rented or hung.

  1. 01

    Venue and stage models

    A to-scale model of the venue, stage and rig from drawings, photos or a site visit, with every fixture in its place.

  2. 02

    Renders and looks

    Stills and clips from the audience, the stage and overhead, in several looks. You pick the direction for each artist, night or genre.

  3. 03

    Spec check

    The production spec checked against the model line by line. Anything unconfirmed is flagged before it is ordered.

  4. 04

    Deliverables

    Final renders, the model file and a fixture list your team can build from.
