Lighting design

Custom lighting design

A lighting rig designed around your venue and your show: fixtures specified, placed, patched, and programmed, then run live alongside the lasers. Available as design only or full production.

Get a quote

Scope

What the design includes

All of this is done before load-in, so show day is execution, not guesswork.

  1. 01

    Rig design

    Fixture selection and placement for your space: moving heads, strobes and blinders, LED pixel bars, wash lights, and atmospherics, with lasers integrated where the show calls for them.

  2. 02

    Plot and patch

    A to-scale lighting plot showing every fixture’s position, DMX address, and mode, plus a patch sheet your crew can build from.

  3. 03

    Programming

    Looks and cues pre-programmed to the music on a console we provide, so lasers and lighting run as one cohesive show.

  4. 04

    Power and cable plan

    Circuit loads, cable runs, and lengths calculated in advance and shared with the venue, so no circuit is overloaded mid-show.

  5. 05

    Build and operation

    We load in, hang and focus the rig, operate it live, and strike the same night. Or your crew can build from our design.

Equipment

Fixture types

Every rig is specified for the venue and the music, never pulled from a one-size-fits-all package.

  • Moving heads

    Beams and spots that pan, tilt, and change color on cue.

  • Strobes and blinders

    High-impact white light for the drop, and blinders that light up the crowd.

  • LED pixel bars

    Linear pixel effects that chase, pulse, and frame the stage.

  • Wash lights

    Even color across the stage, the room, or the dance floor.

  • Fog and haze

    The atmosphere that makes every beam visible.

  • Lasers

    Programmed alongside the lighting for one integrated show.

Deliverables

What you receive

Four production documents, delivered before load-in.

  1. 01

    Lighting plot

    Every fixture drawn to scale in your space, in plan view and from the audience’s perspective.

  2. 02

    Patch sheet

    Each fixture’s DMX address, mode, and cable run, listed in build order.

  3. 03

    Power plan

    Circuit assignments for every fixture, plus the total number of circuits the venue needs to provide.

  4. 04

    Production schedule

    Load-in, focus, doors, show, and strike, scheduled in 30-minute blocks.
