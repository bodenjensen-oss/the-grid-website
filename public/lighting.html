Scope

What a design covers

The work that happens before load-in, so show day is assembly and not guesswork.

  1. 01

    Rig design

    Fixture choice and placement for the space: moving heads, strobes and blinders, LED pixel bars, wash lights, fog and haze, with lasers where the show wants them.

  2. 02

    Plot and patch

    A scaled plot of every fixture, with its position, address and mode, and a patch sheet the crew can build from.

  3. 03

    Programming

    Looks and cues built to the music before show day, on a console we bring, so lasers and lighting play as one show.

  4. 04

    Power and cable plan

    Circuits, cable runs and lengths worked out in advance and handed to the venue, so nothing trips mid-set.

  5. 05

    Build and operation

    We load in, hang and focus the rig, run it live, and pack out the same night. Or your crew builds from the design.

The kit

What goes in a rig

The fixture types we design with. The mix is chosen for the room and the music, not taken from a package.

  • Moving heads

    Beams and spots that pan, tilt and change colour on cue.

  • Strobes and blinders

    The hits: white light on the drop, and light on the crowd.

  • LED pixel bars

    Lines of light that chase, pulse and frame the stage.

  • Wash lights

    Colour across the stage, the room or the dance floor.

  • Fog and haze

    The atmosphere that makes a beam visible.

  • Lasers

    Programmed with the lighting, so both play as one show.

Handoff

What you receive

Four documents, sent before load-in.

  1. 01

    Rig plot

    Every fixture drawn to scale in the space, from above and from the crowd.

  2. 02

    Patch sheet

    Each fixture's address, mode and cable run, in build order.

  3. 03

    Power plan

    Which fixtures share which circuit, and how many circuits the venue needs to provide.

  4. 04

    Schedule

    Load-in, focus, doors, show and strike, in half-hour blocks.
