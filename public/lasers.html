What a laser show covers

Every rig, whatever the size, comes with the same four things.

  1. 01

    Projectors and control

    Full-colour laser projectors with their control system, flown from truss or set on stands, cabled and tested before doors.

  2. 02

    Programming

    Looks built for your music before show day, then played live with the set rather than left on a loop.

  3. 03

    Haze and fog

    Lasers need atmosphere to be seen. We bring the hazers and fog machines and agree the levels with the venue.

  4. 04

    Safety officer on site

    A certified laser safety officer sets the beam zones before doors and stays at the controls for the whole show.
